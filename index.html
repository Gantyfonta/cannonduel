Cannon Duel

Cannon
Duel

Cannons, a hill, three hits to lose. Each barrel swings up and down on its own, so the skill is timing: hold your button to freeze the barrel and charge power, let go to fire. Last cannon standing wins. Watch the wind.

Players

Press any other key on this screen to join. Each player gets a colour, and the map grows with the crowd.

2 barrels

Every cannon gets extra barrels that fire together in a fan.

Ammo

    Every match picks one switched-on projectile and one wind (none, or 1 to 3 pushing left or right) at random, on a freshly generated hill with its own colours and sky. Shots dig round craters where they land.
